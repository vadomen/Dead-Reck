Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Short version: FuGPS does not collect, transmit or sell any data. Everything the app records stays on your iPhone unless you choose to share it yourself.

What the app records

When you start a recording, FuGPS saves the following to files on your device:

  • Location (GPS/GNSS fixes), including while the screen is locked, so the drive has a continuous reference track.
  • Motion sensor data — accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer and barometer readings.
  • Vehicle data from your OBD-II adapter over Bluetooth, such as vehicle speed and engine RPM.

The app asks for permission to use Location, Motion & Fitness and Bluetooth. You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Recording only happens when you start it.

Where the data goes

  • Recordings are stored only in the app's own storage on your iPhone. They are visible to you in the Files app.
  • FuGPS has no servers and no user accounts, and it makes no network requests with your data.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • If you use the Share button to send a recording somewhere (for example AirDrop, Mail or a cloud drive), that copy is handled by the service you chose, under its own privacy policy.

Map downloads

When the app downloads offline road maps, it requests them from this website (fugps.pages.dev), which is hosted by Cloudflare. As with any web request, Cloudflare receives your device's IP address and basic request information to deliver the files; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. The server therefore sees which region's map was downloaded, but that is a region, not your position: the file name names the region (or the national map), and requests are plain downloads. The request contains no recordings, location or other personal data or identifiers, and FuGPS does not keep logs of these downloads.

Maps download over Wi-Fi only, unless you allow cellular data in Settings → Maps. Opening Settings → Maps may fetch the small list of available maps (about 16 KB) over any network, including cellular, at most once a day or when you pull to refresh. The same list is also fetched in the “Road maps” step of the first-launch setup and, when it is due, when you tap Download on the map offer shown at a stop, and once more when a map download finds its file gone from the server.

Recordings contain a precise record of where and when you drove. Share them with care.

Map

The map in FuGPS is Apple Maps, shown with Apple's MapKit. Map tiles are loaded by Apple, and Apple's privacy policy applies to them.

FuGPS keeps its last estimated position on the phone to restore it next time; it never leaves the phone.

Deleting your data

You can delete any recording in the app's Settings → Sessions list or through the Files app. Deleting the app removes all of its recordings from your device. Device backups (for example, iCloud Backup) follow your iOS settings.

Children

FuGPS is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes
