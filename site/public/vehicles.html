Supported cars

FuGPS works with most petrol and diesel cars that have a standard OBD-II port. What matters is that the car reports its speed over OBD-II.

Requirements

OBD-II port

A 16-pin socket, usually under the dashboard near the driver's knees.

Vehicle speed over OBD-II

Standard PID 0D (vehicle speed) is required. Engine RPM (PID 0C) is used if available.

Bluetooth LE adapter

An ELM327-compatible adapter with Bluetooth Low Energy. Classic Bluetooth and Wi-Fi adapters are not supported.

Model years are a guide: OBD-II became mandatory at different times in different markets, and imported cars follow the rules of the market they were built for. Security gateways on newer cars normally do not block the read-only data FuGPS uses.

Supported OBD‑II protocols

The adapter detects the protocol automatically: ISO 15765-4 CAN (11- and 29-bit, 250 and 500 kbit/s), ISO 14230-4 KWP2000, ISO 9141-2, SAE J1850 PWM and VPW.

Safe for your car

FuGPS only reads data. Every request goes through a fixed allowlist of read-only OBD-II commands, so the app cannot clear fault codes, change settings or write anything to the car.
